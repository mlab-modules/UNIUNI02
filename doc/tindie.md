## UNIUNI02: Universal Breadboard PCB for MLAB Kit

### What is it?

The UNIUNI02 is a universal printed circuit board (PCB) with a 2.54mm pitch, designed for use with the MLAB kit system. This versatile PCB allows users to realize their designs using the [Veroroute tool](https://sourceforge.net/projects/veroroute/).

### Why did you make it?

We developed the UNIUNI02A to provide a flexible and compatible perfboard PCB solution for the MLAB kit system. It simplifies the process of prototyping and developing various electronic projects by offering a standard platform that integrates seamlessly with other MLAB modules.

### What makes it special?

The UNIUNI02 is distinguished by its universal design and compatibility with the MLAB system. Key features include:

- **Universal Design**: Suitable for a wide range of electronic projects, making it highly versatile.
- **Standard Pitch**: 2.54mm pitch between holes, ensuring compatibility with standard electronic components and connectors.
- **MLAB Kit Compatibility**: Seamless integration with other MLAB modules, enhancing modularity and expandability.
- **Power Supply Preparation**: Provisions for standard MLAB +5V and +12V power headers, ensuring reliable power delivery.
- **Bus Connectivity Preparation**: Equipped for connecting basic I2C, CAN, or UART TX and RX interfaces, facilitating easy communication between modules.
